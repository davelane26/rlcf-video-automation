The phrase "Pure fire of God" appears in a few different religious contexts:

*   **"Spirit of Grace, Thou Light of Life" (Hymn 192):** This hymn contains the lines, "Pure Fire of God, burn out my sin, / Cleanse all the earthly dross from me"[1][2]. While it does not explicitly use the exact phrase "touch with live coal," the imagery of "pure fire" burning away sin and dross is closely related to the biblical motif of the coal from the altar (Isaiah 6:6–7), which is a common theme in hymns regarding purification.
*   **"Deva Prasanname Irangiyae Vandhiduthae":** This is a Christian song (often found with English and Tamil lyrics) that includes the phrase "Pure fire of God" in its English translation[3][4].
