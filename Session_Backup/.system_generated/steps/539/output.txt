The phrase "Pure fire of God" appears in the lyrics of the hymn **"Burn Fire of God"** (often titled "Burn Fire of God! My Ransomed Soul Possessing"), which has been recorded at the Christian Fellowship Church (CFC) in Bangalore, India[1].

The specific line from the hymn is: **"Pure fire Thou art, and I would dwell in Thee."**[1][2]

While "fire of God" is a common theme in CFC teachings—often used to describe the baptism of the Holy Spirit, the purification of the believer, or God's presence[3][4][5][6]—the exact phrase "Pure fire of God" is most directly associated with this hymn[1].
